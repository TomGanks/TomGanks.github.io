Internship · Designer & Producer

Mermory

A study game where reviewing flashcards earns the time and gear to run a cozy aquarium and go on roguelite fishing expeditions. As designer and producer, I scoped the fishing MVP with a must/should/could/won’t cut list, designed the reel-in mechanic, and pitched how leaderboards, co-op, and a daily story could keep students coming back without pulling them away from learning.

The reel-in mechanicOnce a fish bites, you pull to add tension and steer left or right against it. Early fish only need pulling; later expeditions make you juggle all three while the fish darts around.

A dark pirate action RPG. Vanessa Wyve grew up poor, learned to out-fish and out-smart the market bosses who cheated her, and became a Robin Hood of the seas. Sound design to picture

Final project · 6:23 · Sound Design for Games

Orcs Must Die! Deathtrap, re-sounded

I replaced all the audio on six minutes of four-player co-op gameplay: voices, weapons, abilities, traps, orcs, UI cues, ambience, and music. I cut the footage so each player’s point of view gets its moment.

How I built itI recorded four voice actors in a cardboard-and-blanket booth I built where they live, and let them improvise around each character’s iconic lines. Foley came from a wallet, a water bottle, rings, a cup and spoon, and scissors, shaped with pitch shifting, flanger, and granular effects. Traps got a heavier, blocky low end, and I field-recorded running water with a Zoom recorder for the map’s water mill.

Coursework: gameplay footage from the game, all audio by me and my voice cast

Exam · 1:00

Mobile runner game sounds

How I built itEight synthesized effects in ES2 and Alchemy: merge, level up, level down, rolling, rails, hits, steps, and a win jingle. The level-up pitch climbs as balls keep merging, then resets with the stage. Rising sounds mean good, falling sounds mean bad, so players can hear how they’re doing.

About

Creating pillars, then building worlds on them

I’m Jonathan Silva, a game designer working across systems, story, and sound. I start with the mechanics, loops, and moments of choice that turn one session into a habit, then prototype and playtest until the game feels right.

Music and writing have always been part of how I build. From the futuristic landscapes of sci-fi to old myths and folklore, I want to make games that captivate players and resonate in sound.

Music and writing are connected arts. Each one makes the other stronger.
